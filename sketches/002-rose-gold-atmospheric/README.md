# Variant: Rose Gold Atmospheric

## Design stance
Atmospheric depth with layered radial gradients, subtle glow effects, and a more cinematic feel. The encounter card has an inner highlight line on hover, aura animations on the anonymous presence, and a ring-expand effect on mutual reveal avatar.

## Key choices
- **Layout:** Similar single-column but with more ambient depth
- **Typography:** Same hierarchy, slightly larger mutual lead with text-shadow
- **Color:** Slightly deeper base (#0d090a), richer ambient gradients (three radial layers), stronger glow/shadow on reveal states
- **Interaction:** Hover reveal of top accent line on cards, aura pulse on anonymous presence, expanding ring on mutual avatar, bottom nav with animated indicator bar

## Trade-offs
- Strong at: Atmospheric immersion, cinematic quality, visual depth
- Weak at: Slightly heavier visual weight, more GPU paint (gradients/glows), could feel "moodier"

## Best for
- Evening/night usage contexts
- Users who want a more cinematic, immersive feel
- Brand differentiation through atmosphere
